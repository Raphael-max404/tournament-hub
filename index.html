Go live on the website (free)

Show your game screen to fans and the broadcast, directly from your phone. Ask to go live, wait for the organizer to approve, then start sharing and switch to your game. Keep this page open in the background.

Good to know: fans’ devices connect directly to yours. This can reveal your network address to them, it can use a lot of your data and battery, and it can fail on some networks. Turn on Do Not Disturb first. Nothing is recorded. At most 5 fans and 2 broadcast screens can watch at once.

Show your game on the fan hub
  1. On your phone, open a live-streaming app that can share your screen, such as Twitch or YouTube.
  2. Start a live stream of your game screen.
  3. Copy your live link and paste it below.

Turn on Do Not Disturb first, because notifications can show on the stream. Your link is shown publicly only after the organizer approves it. The streaming app has its own rules and requirements.

How the test works

  1. Tap Start Screen Sharing.
  2. If your device and browser support sharing, the native sharing prompt should appear.
  3. Choose the screen or supported sharing option provided by your device.
  4. If possible, switch to your game and see whether sharing remains active.
  5. Return to Tournament Hub and check the preview.
  6. Stop sharing when finished.

Some mobile browsers may not allow sharing the entire screen, or may stop sharing when you switch apps.
